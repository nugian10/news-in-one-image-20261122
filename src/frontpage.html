Sunday, 22 November 2026
The Gulf — Iran strikes back

IRAN HITS U.S. BASES IN BAHRAIN

MANAMA, BAHRAIN — Missiles and drones arc over the Gulf on Saturday night. A civilian industrial plant burned on Sitra Island; two people were hurt.

Iran’s Revolutionary Guard said its missiles and drones struck two U.S. Patriot air-defence radars at the Al-Riffa and Awali bases. Bahrain said its defences intercepted several attacks and reported no damage to U.S. forces; a U.S. official said no American personnel or facilities were hit.

Climate · Antalya

COP31 collapses with no deal

The UN climate summit in Türkiye ended on Sunday after two days of overtime with no agreement at all. The EU objected to a final text that said nothing about fossil fuels.

Trade · Brussels

EU and Indonesia sign landmark deal

After nearly ten years of talks, the two sides signed a pact scrapping tariffs on up to 98.5% of goods. It takes effect in early 2027, once the European Parliament approves it.

Ukraine · Kryvyi Rih

Missile hits Zelenskyy’s home town

A Russian ballistic missile struck Kryvyi Rih on Saturday, killing a seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman and wounding about 60 people.
